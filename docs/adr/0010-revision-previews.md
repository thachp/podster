# ADR 0010, revision: previews

**Status: Accepted, 2026-11-27, by the owner's answer "Accept all" to eight of the [plan's questions](../m8-preview-plan.md#owner-decisions), recorded as [decisions 250 to 257](../spec/PRODUCT.md#m8-preview-decisions-november-27-2026). Item 11's new secret rests on question 11, which is still open. Nothing is built.** Proposed earlier the same day, and revised after the plan's security review at `33135fc2`. Written for [issue #635](https://github.com/thachp/podster/issues/635). The design, its citations, the build order and the owner's questions are in the [M8 preview plan](../m8-preview-plan.md); this note says only what it changes in [ADR 0010](0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md) and its [revision on apps as Workers](0010-revision-apps-are-workers.md), as the owner accepted it. Where the two ADR documents say otherwise, this note rules on preview hosts; on production hosts they read as written.

## What it changes

1. **A second environment, `preview`, of reviewed, merged commits only.** It reverses the "one environment" part of [decision 214](../spec/PRODUCT.md#decisions-needed), restated in [decision 219](../spec/PRODUCT.md#decisions-needed) and in the revision's "What does not change" (`0010-revision-apps-are-workers.md:402`). It keeps 214's other half: nothing unmerged or unreviewed is served. What is releasable is unchanged (`crates/core/src/release.rs:156-178`). `ENVIRONMENT` (`release.rs:32`) becomes one of two values.
2. **Where content is served** (`0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md:53-117`) gains a second dispatch namespace for previews, a preview label per room drawn from the same never-reused space, one preview slot per room with its own serve state, and a serve-entry form marked `preview:` that carries an access epoch. A production entry and a preview entry can never name each other's script: they dispatch through different namespace bindings.
3. **"The content Worker routes and never authorizes"** (`0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md:109`) becomes "the content Worker enforces only what Rust wrote or signed". On preview hosts it checks Ed25519 signatures made by the private API, with public keys only; it still holds no secret, and decides nothing.
4. **"Podster sets no cookie on any content host and accepts no credential there"** (`0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md:123`) stays true of every production host. **On preview hosts only,** Podster sets two `__Host-` cookies, matched by exact name: a state cookie, and a session of at most an hour bound to that state and checked against it on every request. It accepts its own signed ticket, delivered only in a URL fragment and posted back same-origin by a fixed Podster page, and its marker token. No token is ever in a URL's path or query, and no `/__podster/` request is logged. State-changing requests on a preview host must be same-origin, and answers carry `Origin-Agent-Cluster: ?1`. No cookie is ever sent on to an app, as today (`apps/content/src/handle.ts:248-256`), and the application's own session never leaves its host ([ADR 0001](0001-application-host-and-session-cookie.md)).
5. **The sandbox** (`0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md:144-173`; [decision 216](../spec/PRODUCT.md#decisions-needed)) stays strict on production hosts. **On preview hosts,** the policy adds `allow-same-origin`, because a page with an opaque origin sends no cookie on its own module scripts and `fetch` calls, so no cookie gate could work. The cost and its bounds are the plan's section 5.5. **So a preview doesn't run exactly as production will:** it has an origin, browser storage and same-origin requests with no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`, where production is opaque, has no storage and answers `*` or `null`. The plan's section 5.2.1 lists the differences, and the Ship panel and the approval dialog say them. **Production gate:** no preview is served in production until previews are on a separate registrable domain or their domain is on the Public Suffix List. The owner ruled on both: the narrowing ([Q7](../m8-preview-plan.md#owner-decisions), [decision 255](../spec/PRODUCT.md#decisions-needed)) and the gate ([Q9](../m8-preview-plan.md#owner-decisions), [decision 256](../spec/PRODUCT.md#decisions-needed)), which of the two still being theirs. The production policy's function and its test are left unchanged.
6. **Only a public room releases** (`0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md:44-51`) is unchanged for production. **A private room may have a preview,** served only to people with a role in the room. That is the "signed, expiring link for the room's own people" which [decision 213](../spec/PRODUCT.md#decisions-needed) named and left for its own design.
7. **Rollback** (`0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md:372-381`) is unchanged for production. A preview has none: a new preview of the earlier commit does the same.
8. **Custom domains** (`0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md:383-420`) gain a role on each claim, `production` or `preview`, one of each per room. The proof, the claim's owner object, the recheck and the ending order are unchanged.
9. **Publishing** gains a release made from a preview: it names the preview release, the approval names its build ID, and the production job recomputes the build ID from what it fetched. A publish is a re-upload, since Workers for Platforms dispatches a user Worker by name and has no version selection for it ([limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-for-platforms/workers-for-platforms/reference/limits/)), and a script's store binding is fixed at upload.
10. **Unpublishing** (`0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md:279-310`) deletes the preview on every trigger that unpublishes production, and on the preview switch being off. Changing the room's visibility raises the preview's access epoch instead of deleting it.
11. **Credentials** (`0010-release-and-the-content-domain.md:342-359`) gain one API secret, the ticket signing key. The scripts, routing and store tokens are shared with production, since none can be scoped below the account; the separation is at the app's side: another namespace, another store, another write budget.

## What it does not change

- What a release may contain, the release job's order, its records-before-sends rule and its rechecks.
- The header allow-list on an app's answer, the refusal of service-worker scripts, the domain-wide stop and operator suspension, all of which cover preview hosts.
- Production hosts' answers, byte for byte.
- That a person approves each production release, and an agent never approves.

## The owner's answers, 2026-11-27

The owner was shown eight of the [plan's questions](../m8-preview-plan.md#owner-decisions), each with its recommendation, and the trade-off, and answered "Accept all". That covers Q1 to Q10: the second environment, private-room previews with private production still refused, gated public previews, no agent-made previews yet, previews optional before publishing, the mechanism and the narrowing of decision 216 on staging after spike P1 with the jar-eviction residual accepted there, the production gate, and one preview per room sharing the room's caps. **Still open, for the owner:** Q11, the preview namespace, ticket key and content Worker configuration, and Q12, a pending publish after its preview is replaced. **Also still the owner's:** which production gate under Q9.

## Not verified

Nothing here has run. The browser behaviour it rests on, cookies under each sandbox variant and `__Host-` against a sibling's cookie, is owed to the plan's spike P1 in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
